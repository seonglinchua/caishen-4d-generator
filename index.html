01 / The idea

Make something small, and make it useful.

A Caishen-themed website with one simple purpose: generate a random four-digit number, then copy or share it. A small project became a practical experiment in AI-assisted development.

02 / The collaboration

I set the direction. AI helped build.

I chose the product direction, reviewed browser screenshots, and decided what to refine. Codex helped review the code, separate markup from styles and scripts, and implement the changes.

Together, we made the result easier to read, added copying and sharing, and introduced keyboard focus styles, result announcements, and reduced-motion support.

03 / The review

The browser had the final word.

Screenshots caught what code checks missed: cached styles broke the layout, and generated mascot artwork showed an unwanted background. Versioned assets addressed the stale styles; restoring the original transparent mascot resolved the visible square.

04 / The lesson

Keep the judgment human.

AI helped turn feedback into working revisions. Reviewing the actual experience, questioning the output, and choosing what to keep were still part of my job.

Selected JavaScript behaviors were checked. Full mobile, screen-reader, and cross-browser testing remains to be completed.
